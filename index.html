Can't find it? Grab the EVM address manually

No drama — the manual route is reliable and takes about a minute:

1. Open your Hyperliquid Testnet Portfolio.

2. Find the token in Balances. If it is still on HyperCore, use Transfer to/from EVM and move the amount you want to bridge to HyperEVM first. Elysium bridges the ERC-20 on HyperEVM, not the HyperCore balance.

3. Back in Balances, look at the token's Contract column and tap the small external-link icon.

4. On the token details page, find EVM Address. Copy that full 0x… address.

5. Come back here, paste it below, then hit Check this token. We'll verify your balance, gateway and Elysium representation before anything can be signed.

Why this little bridge? Because doing this by hand in Remix is fun once. This page keeps the exact same bridge flow, minus the ceremony. No backend, no custody: transactions go straight from your wallet to Elysium's bridge contracts.

What actually happens under the hood

The app first asks the official HyperEVM router which gateway handles the token and computes the deterministic Elysium representation address.

You approve only the exact amount being bridged. The Standard Gateway escrows the original ERC-20 on HyperEVM and a cross-chain retryable message delivers a 1:1 representation on Elysium.

On the first bridge, the Elysium representation can be deployed automatically at its deterministic address. Later deposits mint against the same representation. Delivery fees are paid in native testnet HYPE.

If automatic delivery fails, the original deposit should not be sent again: the existing retryable can be retried before it expires.
